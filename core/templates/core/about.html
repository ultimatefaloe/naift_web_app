Our story

The School of Information and Communication Technology (SICT) was created in the 2009/2010 academic session. The pioneer departments were Computer Science, Cyber Security Science, Information and Media Technology, and Library and Information Technology. SICT received the National Universities Commission's Pre-Accreditation Resource Verification Visit in 2010, which saw all its departments accredited. Since its inception, the School has graduated over 1,000 undergraduates and 500 postgraduates.

Department of Information Technology

The Department of Information Technology was founded in 2021 under the School of Information and Communication Technology (SICT). The department trains students in the knowledge, skills, and practices needed to design, build, deploy, and manage the information systems that organizations rely on every day.
